<h1 class="page-title">Biography</h1>

<p class="lead"><strong>Alireza Taheritajar</strong> is a Ph.D. student in <a href="https://www.augusta.edu/ccs/">Computer Science and Cyber Security</a> at <a href="https://www.augusta.edu/">Augusta University</a>, where he works as a Graduate Research Assistant at the <a href="https://www.jeoresearch.com/ccslab">ARVR Lab</a> under the supervision of <a href="https://www.jeoresearch.com/research">Dr. Jason Orlosky</a> and <a href="https://jieqiongzhao.github.io/">Dr. Jieqiong Zhao</a>. His research lies at the intersection of <strong>computer vision</strong>, <strong>artificial intelligence</strong>, and <strong>mixed reality</strong>, with an emphasis on <strong>incremental learning</strong> and <strong>real-time 3D object understanding</strong> for XR systems.</p>

<p>His work has been published at venues including IEEE ISMAR, IEEE AIxVR, CANS, ICICS, and the Journal of Real-Time Image Processing, and he has presented live demonstrations at ICCV 2025 and CVPR 2026. In 2026 he received Augusta University’s Award of Excellence in Research. He serves as Publicity Chair for IEEE AIxVR 2026 and as a reviewer for IEEE ISMAR and IEEE AIxVR.</p>

<h2 class="subsection-title">Industry Experience</h2>

<p>Prior to joining Augusta University, he was a <strong>Machine Learning Engineer and Technical Lead</strong> at <a href="https://aiex.ca/">AIEX</a> in Montreal, Canada (remote), where he led the development of a no-code computer vision platform for dataset generation, model training, and real-time deployment on server and edge devices. He also designed and optimized visual inspection systems using YOLO, Mask R-CNN, and related deep learning models.</p>

<p>Before that, he co-founded and technically led startups focused on <strong>Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)</strong> and <strong>industrial automation</strong>. This work involved designing and implementing vehicle detection and classification systems using inductive loop sensors, wireless magnetic technologies, and computer vision, contributing to smart traffic monitoring and automated inspection solutions deployed in real-world road infrastructure.</p>

<h2 class="subsection-title">Education</h2>

<p>He received his <strong>M.Sc.</strong> and <strong>B.Sc.</strong> degrees in <strong>Electrical and Control Engineering</strong> from <a href="https://basu.ac.ir/en">Bu-Ali Sina University</a> in Hamedan, Iran, where he graduated as a top student. His master’s thesis focused on vehicle counting and speed detection using computer vision, and his undergraduate project involved designing a camera robot for real-time visual tracking.</p>

<h2 class="subsection-title">Leadership</h2>

<p>Beyond research, he has been active in academic leadership, serving as Vice President of the Computer &amp; Cyber Graduate Student Organization at Augusta University and previously leading multiple robotics and entrepreneurship initiatives at Bu-Ali Sina University.</p>
