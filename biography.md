<h1 class="page-title">Biography</h1>

<p class="lead"><strong>Alireza Taheritajar</strong> is a Ph.D. student in <a href="https://www.augusta.edu/ccs/">Computer and Cyber Science</a> at <a href="https://www.augusta.edu/">Augusta University</a>, where he works as a Graduate Research Assistant at the <a href="https://www.jeoresearch.com/ccslab">ARVR Lab</a> under the supervision of <a href="https://www.jeoresearch.com/aboutme.html">Dr. Jason Orlosky</a> and <a href="https://jieqiongzhao.github.io/">Dr. Jieqiong Zhao</a>.</p>

<p>His research focuses on <strong>intelligent mixed reality systems</strong> that integrate <strong>real-time 3D perception</strong>, <strong>multimodal large language models</strong>, and <strong>adaptive human-AI interaction</strong>. He investigates context-aware embodied AI agents that perceive and reason about the physical world, understand user intent, and adapt their behavior and interfaces to support human-AI collaboration. His work includes incremental learning and one-shot 3D annotation for object detection on AR headsets, and retrieval-augmented object detection and guidance in augmented reality.</p>

<p>His research has been published at IEEE ISMAR, IEEE AIxVR, CANS, ICICS, and the Journal of Real-Time Image Processing, and he has presented peer-reviewed live demonstrations at ICCV 2025 and CVPR 2026. He was accepted to the IEEE VIS 2026 Doctoral Colloquium. In 2026 he received the Augusta University Graduate School’s Award of Excellence in Research, following its Integrity Award in 2024. During his first year at Augusta University (2023–2024), he conducted research on acoustic side-channel attacks under the supervision of Dr. Reza Rahaeimehr.</p>

<h2 class="subsection-title">Industry Experience</h2>

<p>From 2021 to 2023, he was a <strong>Machine Learning Engineer and Technical Lead</strong> at <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/aiex-ai">AIEX</a> in Montreal, Canada, where he managed a team of engineers building a no-code computer vision platform for dataset generation, model training, and edge deployment. He directed visual inspection deployments using YOLO and Mask R-CNN pipelines across several industrial sites.</p>

<p>Earlier, he worked on <strong>intelligent transportation systems</strong> and <strong>industrial automation</strong> in Hamedan, Iran. As Technical Manager at Sina ATI NET (2017–2020), he led the design and deployment of vehicle detection systems based on inductive loops and wireless magnetic sensors for highway monitoring. He co-founded Atitek Faradid Pars (2015–2017), an industrial automation robotics company, and worked as a robotics engineer at RoboSina (2014–2016), building autonomous robots for national competitions. He holds three Iranian patents, including an apparatus for vehicle classification via inductive loops.</p>

<h2 class="subsection-title">Education</h2>

<p>He received his <strong>M.Sc. in Electrical and Control Engineering</strong> (2019) and <strong>B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering</strong> (2016) from <a href="https://basu.ac.ir/en">Bu-Ali Sina University</a> in Hamedan, Iran, graduating in the top 5% and top 10% of his classes, respectively. His master’s thesis addressed counting, classifying, and detecting the speed of vehicles using computer vision, and his undergraduate thesis covered the design and fabrication of a camera robot.</p>

<h2 class="subsection-title">Service &amp; Leadership</h2>

<p>He serves as Publicity Chair for IEEE AIxVR 2026 and 2027, has served on the Executive Board of Intercollegiate XR (ICXR), and reviews for IEEE ISMAR and IEEE AIxVR. At Augusta University he is Treasurer of the AI/ML Paper Reading Group and previously served as Vice President of the Computer and Cyber Graduate Student Organization. At Bu-Ali Sina University he headed the Entrepreneurship Committee and the Electrical &amp; Robotics Student Committee.</p>
