<h2 id="about-title" class="section-title">About Me</h2>

<p class="lead">I am a Ph.D. student in <a href="https://www.augusta.edu/ccs/">Computer Science and Cyber Security</a> at <a href="https://www.augusta.edu/">Augusta University</a>, where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant at the <a href="https://www.jeoresearch.com/ccslab">ARVR Lab</a> under the supervision of <a href="https://www.jeoresearch.com/research">Dr. Jason Orlosky</a> and <a href="https://jieqiongzhao.github.io/">Dr. Jieqiong Zhao</a>.</p>

<p>My research lies at the intersection of <strong>computer vision</strong>, <strong>artificial intelligence</strong>, and <strong>mixed reality</strong>, with an emphasis on <strong>incremental learning</strong> and <strong>real-time 3D object understanding</strong> for XR systems. My recent work has appeared at IEEE ISMAR, IEEE AIxVR, and CANS, and I have presented live demos at ICCV 2025 and CVPR 2026.</p>

<p>Before my Ph.D., I received my M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees in Electrical and Control Engineering from <a href="https://basu.ac.ir/en">Bu-Ali Sina University</a>, Hamedan, Iran, and worked in industry as a machine learning engineer and technical lead on computer vision systems. Read my <a href="{{ '/biography/' | relative_url }}">full biography</a>.</p>
